Opportunity scoring

How the Revenue Trigger Opportunity Score works.

The Revenue Trigger Opportunity Score ranks each opportunity from 0–100 based on factors like project type, scope, commercial relevance, estimated value, and recency—so you can quickly see which opportunities deserve attention first.

75–100 · HOT

High-priority opportunity with strong timing, scope and seller-fit signals.

60–74 · WARM

Good opportunity worth active review.

40–59 · WATCH

Potential opportunity with weaker scope, timing or commercial relevance.

0–39 · LOW

Low-priority activity remains visible rather than being artificially rated warm.

0–25Project / permit type
0–20Scope intensity
0–15Commercial relevance
0–15Permit stage
0–15Economic size, using official valuation where available
0–5Seller breadth
0–5Recency
0 to −20Negative adjustments for canceled, expired, low-relevance or limited-scope activity
Seller Fit: trade-specific relevance is calculated separately for HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing, Roofing, Landscaping, Security and Signage.
Important: this is an explainable ranking model, not a prediction of project award or guaranteed spend.
