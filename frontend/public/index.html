Opportunity scoring

How the RevenueTrigger Opportunity Score works.

The RevenueTrigger Opportunity Score ranks each opportunity from 0–100 based on factors like project type, scope, commercial relevance, estimated value, and recency—so you can quickly see which opportunities deserve attention first.

80–100 · HOT

High-priority opportunity with strong timing, scope and seller-fit signals.

60–79 · WARM

Good opportunity worth active review.

40–59 · WATCH

Potential opportunity with weaker scope, timing or commercial relevance.

0–39 · LOW

Low-priority activity remains visible rather than being artificially rated warm.

0–15Commercial relevance
